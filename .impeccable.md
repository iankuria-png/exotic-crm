## Design Context

### Users

Developers and technical operators use CRM Error Logs to identify which failures affect the system, assess urgency, and drill into an incident without scanning a long administrative table.

### Brand Personality

Technical, composed, and evidence-led. The interface should create confidence and focus rather than alarm fatigue.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the existing Pulse-inspired operational language: a dark, high-signal observability surface with disciplined semantic status color, tabular numbers, and clear incident hierarchy. Avoid a generic settings form or a decorative dashboard.

### Design Principles

1. Put active incidents and their operational impact ahead of configuration controls.
2. Make severity, recurrence, and recency scannable in seconds.
3. Keep filters compact and progressive; preserve full technical detail on demand.
4. Use color only as semantic state, with accessible text and icon support.
5. Retain responsive, keyboard-accessible controls and low-motion interaction.
