Coverage against the received payments — 10 November backtest

The reviewed corpus has 183 confirmed primary TZ purchases. The approved TZS35,000 default plus the unique current TZS40,000 catalogue match correctly describe 174/183 (95.1%): 167 VIP 7-day purchases at TZS35,000 and 7 PREMIUM 14-day purchases at TZS40,000. Four additional TZS35,000 purchases were VIP8days; five were noncatalogue/ambiguous amounts. This is package coverage, not a claim that 95% could already activate without knowing the client.

A separate registered SELECT-only simulation scanned 9,324 positive successful TZ cash records and replayed matching as a read-only calculation, excluding each current receipt's cash and all later payment times. It used current client profiles, which are not historical profile snapshots. The broader “unique full advert phone OR one prior payer-cash relationship” approach selected 58 client candidates from the 183 labels; 7 selected the wrong client. With the two amount defaults it would attempt 56 package/client selections: 49 agree with staff, 7 do not (six wrong-client selections and one wrong 7-day versus 8-day package). The seventh wrong-client selection among all58 had no default package and is outside those56 attempts. Using the last paid package blindly made package mistakes too. These are simulated selections before WordPress/profile/tier readiness; they are not 49 guaranteed completed automatic activations. Lowering the threshold alone is insufficient.

For the eight new pilot receipts, observed worker evidence proves receipt206 matched both stages and was blocked by secondary approval. The broader simulation finds four client/package candidates (204/205/206/208), but 205 uses the current client cache and 208 had a saved incomplete-history hold. Thus 1/8 is directly proven by saved worker evidence; 4/8 is a candidate ceiling for these wider lookup rules, not a verified completion count. Receipt204 is already staff-activated and must never be reactivated. Four other receipts have no identifying phone/profile/history candidate in this calculation; package prices alone cannot name their customer.

The stronger improvement is to learn from real staff-confirmed SMS payer → client outcomes, rather than the legacy payment phone. Retain the direct-lookup simulation as a baseline and evaluate the learned rule chronologically, allowing only staff confirmations made before each incoming SMS. Proposed rules and authority

Keep client identity scoring, funds checks, future-only intake epoch, one-hour age, account/site rollout and exposure limits independent from package inference. A package history match must never increase its own client score.

Offer amount defaults only after at least five distinct receipts from five clients, 100% agreement on an exact currently eligible ProductPrice, and no observed alternate tier/duration in the preceding 90 days of captured, wallet-linked staff-confirmed receipts only. Legacy context is never used for amount-default counts. Show qualifying/excluded counts, window, last confirmation, each alternative, and source types. Only a manager’s explicit Rules save makes a default authoritative. Existing approved defaults remain explicit decisions even when history is mixed. Recompute under the account lock at save; stale evidence/version returns 409. No catalogue row is changed.

For client repeat matching, one clean staff-confirmed SMS receipt is sufficient evidence under one manager-approved learning policy. Do not make staff separately approve every client or repeat purchase. A qualifying relationship is the actual observed SMS payer fingerprint → the exact client confirmed for that receipt. It must have complete payer evidence, matching cash amount/currency, one primary client, verified staff activation of that exact payment/deal and exact staff confirmation, and no correction, dispute, reversal or conflicting recipient. A successful legacy payment's phone field alone is not this authority. Legacy receipts already recorded outside Collections qualify through their exact reference/alias, matching amount/currency, successful live cash, exact client/deal reverse links and exact staff activation timeline/audit for that payment. A CollectionBenefitEffect is not required for legacy evidence; none exists live. For new Collection-owned purchases, use the verified effect plus real staff confirmation. Freeze the source IDs, actor, confirmation time, observed payer and exact package facts/digest at decision time; reject unresolved source changes. Review all qualifying source receipts for that payer: multiple clients hold the current receipt for staff. Missing payer identity also remains an exception.

For each incoming receipt, the evidence reader excludes that receipt, any source confirmed at or after the current provider timestamp, and every automatic outcome not explicitly confirmed/reviewed by real staff. The chronological backtest uses the same cutoff, so a later manual match cannot explain an earlier automatic decision. The evidence reader remains a pure query. It never manufactures a manually reviewed payer-link row or claims the confirming staff member separately approved a standing agreement. Record the manager's learning-policy authority and the source receipt/actual staff confirmation independently in the automatic decision audit. CollectionMatchService can select that independently established payer relationship; a history-derived package intent cannot supply its own identity evidence. Give one clean observed-SMS/staff-confirmed relationship a rule score of 95 under the approved learning policy; the existing strict score > match_threshold rule stays in force (currently80). Conflicting legacy payment-phone-only clues are displayed for review but do not overrule a unique observed-SMS/staff-confirmed relationship under this explicit policy. Missing/incomplete observed payer evidence still holds. This precedence is tested against the seven incorrect legacy-phone selections in the baseline; no name-only or package-only identity selection is added.

Once identity is independently established, use that client's last staff-confirmed purchase at the exact amount/currency in the preceding 90 days, provided current catalogue price, tier and days still match and the bounded history contains no incompatible package choice at that amount. Use the actual most recent eligible ProductPrice; identical tier/duration across 143 and 256 need not force another manual approval. Keep material 7-day/8-day contradictions in review rather than choosing the most frequent package. A missing, disabled or discounted/noncatalogue price remains a staff choice. Package evidence is bounded by the 90-day source window and exact amount; every new transfer is still a separate receipt and a single cash/entitlement operation.
