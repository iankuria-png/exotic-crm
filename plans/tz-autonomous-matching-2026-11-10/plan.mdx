## Proposed rules and authority

Keep client identity scoring, funds checks, future-only intake epoch, one-hour age, account/site rollout and exposure limits independent from package inference. A package history match must never increase its own client score.

Offer amount defaults only after **at least five distinct receipts from five clients, 100% agreement on an exact currently eligible ProductPrice, and no observed alternate tier/duration** in the preceding 90 days of captured, wallet-linked staff-confirmed receipts only. Legacy context is never used for amount-default counts. Show qualifying/excluded counts, window, last confirmation, each alternative, and source types. Only a manager’s explicit Rules save makes a default authoritative. Existing approved defaults remain explicit decisions even when history is mixed. Recompute under the account lock at save; stale evidence/version returns 409. No catalogue row is changed.

For client repeat matching, **one clean staff-confirmed SMS receipt is sufficient evidence** under one manager-approved learning policy. Do not make staff separately approve every client or repeat purchase. A qualifying relationship is the actual observed SMS payer fingerprint → the exact client confirmed for that receipt. It must have complete payer evidence, matching cash amount/currency, one primary client, verified staff activation of that exact payment/deal and exact staff confirmation, and no correction, dispute, reversal or conflicting recipient. A successful legacy payment's `phone` field alone is not this authority. Legacy receipts already recorded outside Collections qualify through their exact reference/alias, matching amount/currency, successful live cash, exact client/deal reverse links and exact staff activation timeline/audit for that payment. A `CollectionBenefitEffect` is **not** required for legacy evidence; none exists live. For new Collection-owned purchases, use the verified effect plus real staff confirmation. Freeze the source IDs, actor, confirmation time, observed payer and exact package facts/digest at decision time; reject unresolved source changes. Review all qualifying source receipts for that payer: multiple clients hold the current receipt for staff. Missing payer identity also remains an exception.

For each incoming receipt, the evidence reader excludes that receipt, any source confirmed at or after the current provider timestamp, and every automatic outcome not explicitly confirmed/reviewed by real staff. The chronological backtest uses the same cutoff, so a later manual match cannot explain an earlier automatic decision. The evidence reader remains a pure query. It never manufactures a manually reviewed payer-link row or claims the confirming staff member separately approved a standing agreement. Record the manager's learning-policy authority and the source receipt/actual staff confirmation independently in the automatic decision audit. `CollectionMatchService` can select that independently established payer relationship; a history-derived **package** intent cannot supply its own identity evidence. Give one clean observed-SMS/staff-confirmed relationship a rule score of **95** under the approved learning policy; the existing strict `score > match_threshold` rule stays in force (currently80). Resolve explicit reviewed links and authoritative observed-SMS mappings before weak legacy `Payment.phone` clues. Two different clients in authoritative payer evidence remain a conflict capped at49. Conflicting legacy payment-phone-only clues are displayed for review but do not overrule a unique observed-SMS/staff-confirmed relationship under this explicit policy. Missing/incomplete observed payer evidence still holds. This precedence is tested against the seven incorrect legacy-phone selections in the baseline; no name-only or package-only identity selection is added.

Once identity is independently established, use that client's last staff-confirmed purchase at the exact amount/currency in the preceding 90 days, provided current catalogue price, tier and days still match and the bounded history contains no incompatible package choice at that amount. Use the actual most recent eligible ProductPrice; identical tier/duration across 143 and 256 need not force another manual approval. Keep material 7-day/8-day contradictions in review rather than choosing the most frequent package. A missing, disabled or discounted/noncatalogue price remains a staff choice. Package evidence is bounded by the 90-day source window and exact amount; every new transfer is still a separate receipt and a single cash/entitlement operation. Automatic decisions awaiting review cannot train later decisions.

Introduce the explicit machine intent authority `matched_receipt_history` in `CollectionPurchaseService` with separate validation of source receipt IDs/digests, policy version/effective time, observed payer/client identity, current exact price and receipt amount. Extend `CollectionMatchService::evidence()` to exclude both `matched_catalogue` and `matched_receipt_history` from intent-based identity-score uplift. This avoids the existing standing-agreement requirement for a separately ticked `auto_allowed` payer link without impersonating staff or weakening existing standing agreements. Each generated history intent is **single-receipt**: `provider_reference` equals the current receipt reference, `max_renewals=1`, `used_count=0`, `valid_from=max(policy.approved_at, all source confirmation times)` (must be at or before provider time), and `valid_until=provider_at + max_auto_age_seconds + 1 second`. `approved_by/approved_at` identify the actual manager learning-policy authority; source staff confirmation is separately attributed. Freeze source receipt/payment/deal IDs, source confirmation timestamps, observed payer fingerprint, current target identity and current exact ProductPrice terms/digest. Under the account lock, reuse the same unconsumed intent for the same receipt/evidence digest; duplicate enrichment/jobs must not mint another authority. `CollectionPurchaseService::validate()` adds `matched_receipt_history` to supported authorities, revalidates source/policy/current terms and independent learned-payer identity, and treats only this validated machine branch like `matched_catalogue` for the existing non-catalogue payer-link requirement. It must not fall into the existing generic `identity_authority=rule_supported_payer` rejection or automatic `reviewed_unique_payer_required` branch. Revoked/changed/expired sources hold for staff; old epochs and already-owned receipts cannot become new dispatches. Existing staff-reviewed quotes and standing agreements retain their current authority and validation unchanged.

Selection order: current explicit staff quote/agreement first; then the independently supported, noncontradictory client-specific package; then the approved amount default; then an existing unique exact catalogue price. Known material client-package conflicts stop fallback to a global default. Multiple explicit current authorities remain staff choice. The worker cannot replace a staff quote with learned history.
