## Outcome and existing failure

The request from 12 November 20:00 EAT through 13 November 05:09 EAT must query 12 November17:00UTC through13November02:09UTC, excluding the upper endpoint. The current dashboard adapter defaults to30days without window=custom, truncates explicit bounds to whole days and rejects hourly grouping for arbitrary windows. Its subscription collections exclude contact-unlock and other service purposes, so that figure cannot represent consolidated collections.

## Approach

Reuse Payment::reportableSuccessful for live/business status, sandbox/test, manual-review, reversal and bundle visibility rules. Add an MCP-specific bounded reporting service rather than changing the established dashboard HTTP contract. Preserve default summary metrics and append an explicit collections section; explicit bounds return exact subscription and visitor metrics plus the same collections section. Customer-mix or active-snapshot metrics not calculated for exact windows are labelled unavailable. New service totals carry native amounts, cached USD equivalents, FX gaps, retained-record and provider-assurance caveats. New-cash collections exclude wallet-funded purchases; a separately labelled earned-service total includes wallet redemptions, while top-ups are excluded from both. Every country/platform/category group carries both totals. The occurrence clock is completed_at, falling back to confirmed_at and finally created_at with explicit fallback counts; no provider-settlement claim is inferred. Renewal uses immutable payment.subscription_lifecycle, and missing lifecycle stays subscription with a classification caveat. Receipt matching uses provider, environment, currency, receiving-account provenance where present and platform fallback when absent, across retained eligible records before applying the report window; ambiguous counts/native amounts are exposed. Committed split-bundle allocations remain additive. Rollback drops only the new view and reverts owned source/config; existing SQL enablement/grants are preserved. New view allowlisting must be reconciled with stored hatch overrides and the SELECT-only account grant after deployment.
