Design

Compute a query-local DENSE_RANK over the full retained candidate universe ordered by normalised receipt, currency, provider and environment, then LEFT JOIN the two ranked candidate relations by their numeric receipt_group. DENSE_RANK preserves the database's own collation/equality rules, including Unicode equivalence and padding; no string truncation or binary hash is used. Both ranks are computed before country/date/platform filtering so cross-site and outside-window collisions remain detectable. Windowed derived relations materialize with a small numeric lookup key even when a UTF-8 receipt exceeds temporary string-index limits. Keep every collision predicate unchanged: different payment ID; nonempty normalised receipt; matching currency/provider/environment; known receiving account across sites or same-site fallback when either account is missing; different economic identity or repeated/null split-bundle sequence. Select only reported fields and an included/ambiguous disposition, with outer DISTINCT so multiple collisions never duplicate an economic row. The numeric equality join lets MariaDB build a small temporary lookup key. The internal group is a query-only cohort and is never exposed as a stable/public identifier; presenters and aggregate view select their existing fields. Verify the actual plan and runtime; do not infer improvement from SQL shape alone.

The dashboard will call RevenueReportingService once and present summary/trend/market outputs from that same in-memory report. Standalone tool presenters retain their argument-based methods. Reuse is explicit within one request, without persistent financial caching, a cross-user cache or long-lived service state. Preserve per-payment native/USD rounding and cached-only FX.

The existing stored SQL view keeps its equivalent eligibility semantics. This correction targets typed MCP queries; it does not recreate or migrate the existing view. Its standalone production-volume performance remains a separate limitation. Reverting the scoped source restores the previous execution path without schema/data changes.
