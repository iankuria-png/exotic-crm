Native currencies in weekly executive scorecards

Let recipients inspect original payment currencies and week comparisons in the existing scorecard and exports, then deploy the isolated release.

Status authorized for implementation and shipping by IanOwner Ian KuriaDate 2026-11-06Scope weekly shared scorecard, native payment aggregates, PDF and MarkdownNon-goals payment or exchange-rate policy changes; regenerating reports or sending messages

The compact currency control sits between navigation and metrics. Reporting USD stays the initial view; Native currencies reveals a currency picker and original revenue, daily averages and comparisons. Market and staff revenue retains every original currency separately. Counts remain the same. Archived recovery amounts keep their source-currency labels; globally combined CFA source breakdowns are shown as recorded and never relabelled XOF/XAF without context. Commentary, portfolio rankings and churn estimates retain their explicit reporting basis. Switching is immediate, with restrained color transitions, stable focus and reduced-motion support.
