# Payment journey refinement · 9 November

Ian reiterated the core job: receive the customer's payment SMS, identify the CRM client, match the purchased package, and activate autonomously when the agreed criteria pass. Staff handle exceptions. The settings form must not dominate the ordinary payment workspace.

This refines the accepted implementation; it does not replace its eligibility, ownership, correction or Finance contracts.

- Put the incoming queue before administration. Keep phone health and automatic status compact, and provide a focused link to settings.
- Show SMS receipt → client match → package match → actual activation confirmation. A match or saved purchase never establishes a successful site change.
- Keep profile links, scores and reasons visible. Queue scores describe the last saved assessment; drawer suggestions describe current evidence; automatic activations retain their original decision evidence.
- Assess the package while automation is paused using a pure preview shared with the actual selector. Never mint purchase authority, reserve exposure, contact WordPress or activate from a read request.
- Prefill a unique policy-supported package for staff review; keep ambiguous identity/package choices explicit. TZS 35,000 keeps the approved standard VIP · 7 days default.
- Keep the primary manual action visible in the drawer footer, including mobile and keyboard navigation. Keep optional renewal agreements secondary.
- Prove the intake/outbox path through identity, approved default, site write, lost-response recovery and duplicate delivery. Preserve one receipt, one allocation and one new cash row.

Release boundary: a scoped CRM code/read-model/UI overlay and matching built assets, with exact previews, private originals, rollback, independent runtime/asset/auth/receipt/worker checks. No migration, historical replay, live customer activation, Finance acceptance or automatic enablement is part of this refinement. The approved >80 score, one-hour age and future-only rules remain unchanged. The 24-hour observation exception starts only at actual authorized enablement after the other acceptance checks pass.
