Autonomously activate clear office-phone payments, then review and correct

Recognize clear incoming payments, autonomously activate the correct purchased subscription and linked exposure, then let sales review, correct and reconcile the result.

Recommendation

The intended default is payment arrives → identify customer → identify purchased package → activate autonomously → sales review afterward. Use the Ghana and TZ office Android phones as private recipient-event sources. Combine messages for the same receipt, resolve reviewed payer/purchase context, reuse or create the correct paid subscription and apply one verified entitlement effect. Clear cases need no rep to copy a code, choose a package or press Activate. Sales works a review-after-activation queue and prefilled exceptions, with undo/reassignment from Payments and Client Detail. Shadow capture is a validation stage before enabling this mode, not the product's final operating model.

Ian confirmed both phones are Android and physically in the office. Ghana's configured manual method identifies MTN. The supplied TZ XML now shows Vodacom M-PESA receiving-message families with thousands of exact TZ #6 CRM reference links. It identifies the principal observed rail; device/SIM/account ownership and current binding must still be verified at setup. Tanzania #2 has a separate configured Vodacom method. TZ #6 is the paid primary; Tanzania #2 receives free exposure for the same paid period. Keep separate profile identities and one linked entitlement bundle, with a single paid receipt on TZ.

For example, a Ghana advertiser chooses a GHS 380 product or tells a rep which product to activate. A recipient-side credit appears on the office phone. CRM checks its account, currency, principal, finality and receipt identity, then links it to the existing GHS 380 payment claim. For an eligible clear match, the system activates once and the rep sees “Auto-activated · Review needed”. If the profile was already activated when proof was uploaded, the same receipt closes verification without adding subscription days.

The long-term system is a collection and reconciliation layer with interchangeable inputs: official provider events first where an existing collection account supports them, Android SMS capture where needed, and statement imports for gaps. This keeps current customer payment habits usable.

Autonomous decision policy and historical cutover

The engine evaluates four separate questions: Is this eligible receiving evidence? Which exact profile? Which authorized purchased package? Can the benefit be applied safely once? A clear decision needs all four. An owned current intent is the strongest path. For repeat behavior, use an explicitly reviewed payer/profile link and a valid standing renewal agreement, reinforced by recent unambiguous history; sales does not create a fresh deal for every renewal. The system creates/reuses the quote/deal snapshot under that authority. A standing agreement specifies exact profile/market, tier/product/duration, final agreed amount/currency, stacking, validity and revocation. History alone, a public advert phone or a copied receipt code is not such authority. New negotiated prices and changed packages obtain missing authorization in the exception drawer.

Freeze the decision-rule version before time-separated shadow evaluation against adjudicated intent cases, including deliberate profile switches, shared payers, different duration at the same amount and changed agreements. Report customer and package errors independently and jointly; retained CRM labels are leads for adjudication, not unquestioned truth. Never calculate automatic coverage from reference-link rate or call the exploratory parse rate production accuracy.

All supplied XML events are historical/reconciliation-only. Do not replay the 2024–2026 archive into an activation worker. At live enrollment, pin the server cutover instant, device/SIM/account mapping and durable inbox cursor. Process an overlap for deduplication and reconciliation, but a pre-cutover provider transaction remains historical even if its SMS arrives after cutover. Unknown provider time or cutover ambiguity holds for review. Historical receipts can propose links and reveal missing ledger data; service recovery is separately reviewed through the existing recovery workflow. Authenticated new live delivery enters account-scoped policy only after the target WP contract/all-writer gates and caps pass.

Receiving accounts and events

Create a collection account identity separate from a platform: country, provider, actual receiving wallet/bank account and ownership. Maintain account-to-platform bindings with effective dates and the complete historical destination snapshot. A shared account creates candidate platforms, never an automatic country-wide grant. Enrol each office SIM/subscription to its account; detect SIM replacement, slot changes and provider changes before accepting automatic attribution. Store contact numbers and subscriber identity privately, with restricted encrypted fields. The human-entered provider label is not enough to authorize an account.

Use two duplicate layers: transport (device_id, external_event_id) for phone retries; canonical provider receipt (country, provider, provider_account_scope, provider_transaction_id_norm) for SMS/API/statement convergence. Country/currency namespace rules are explicit; currency mismatch on the same canonical receipt is a conflict, not a new spendable receipt. Reference aliases can map different sender/bank/wallet IDs only through observed provider/statement evidence or a finance-reviewed mapping. Missing/ambiguous IDs remain in review. Reversal/correction observations append to the same receipt and can arrive before the original credit.

A durable raw-event insert and processing outbox must commit before HTTP acknowledgement. A duplicated ID with changed content is quarantined and logged as a conflict. Parser success is independent of ingest success. Unknown events can be acknowledged as durably quarantined so retries do not overwhelm the queue. Store provider time, SMS-receive time and server-ingest time separately.

Purchase matching

The best match is an authenticated purchase intent or an existing manual submission tied to the exact client/profile, product, duration, destination snapshot, final price and currency. Add an intent when the rep selects a product; preserve the signed customer identity for a self-service purchase. For a new or ambiguous payer, a payment arriving without an intent stays unmatched until an owned-account claim or staff review. A proven recurring payer/purchase pattern may create an inferred renewal intent under the policy below. Prefer a matching provider reference or proven alias; raw phone/name/amount/time similarities only rank suggestions. A bare reference claim is insufficient for automatic activation because another person may know it.

Automatic decisions can use a provider-bound order reference or a unique, current, explicitly reviewed payer→profile relationship plus a clear purchase pattern and approved exposure policy. The proposed historical candidate screen uses at least three earlier positive payments within 90 days, but it does not authorize activation. The real TZ replay shows 39/671 profile disagreements against retained CRM labels even for that simple rule. Automatic use requires a separately reviewed current payer/profile relationship and a valid current intent or standing purchase/renewal agreement, plus no conflicting claim or unresolved correction/reversal. Those are contextual authority, not a repetition count. Reviewed historical patterns propose the standing agreement once; later eligible renewals need no new sales-created deal. A receiving account without a complete payer identity and without an order reference stays assisted. Receipt-code entry or screenshot OCR alone stays assisted. Multiple profiles, a changed pattern or disputed history produces a ranked choice; existing linked subsidiary exposure is not a second customer candidate. Third-party payers are first-class identities. Use a reviewed payer→profile relationship and current purchase context; never activate whichever advertiser phone merely resembles the sender.

Exact receiver principal/currency/eligible time window/account must match the final authorized quote, approved negotiated package or current standing renewal agreement. Infer product/discount proposals from context; final fulfillment requires an existing approval or policy-authorized recurring negotiated quote, never an invented exchange rate or silently accepted short payment. Expired quotes, conflicting claims, multiple candidates, changed destination and unsupported purposes are held. Split/partial/combined/overpaid receipts remain finance-reviewed using existing bundle/root semantics; no automatic allocation or subscription extension. Late evidence for an already activated purchase verifies/reconciles that purchase without granting again. An existing manual ledger payment is linked before considering a new Payment row, preventing double revenue.

Historical/manual/imported records and provider callbacks must all consult the receipt reservation and client operation guard for enrolled accounts, including queued jobs and staff edits. Before enabling writes, map prior receipts/root allocations and identify ambiguous historical references; unresolved overlap is held. Unknown old destinations cannot be globally matched by reference. Reuse the existing platform reference-conflict checks but add the shared-account/global receipt scope they currently lack.

Correct a wrong match without starting over

Place Confirm correct, Change client or package, and Undo this activation on the payment drawer and the linked Client Detail entry. “Undo this activation” means compensate this purchase effect while retaining received money in an unassigned state; it is not a bank refund or deletion of the profile. Existing Client Detail “Revert profile” removes the public profile and must not be reused. Existing SubscriptionDeactivationService deactivates the profile and can mark the payment reversed/high risk; it is not a safe implementation of a mistaken-match correction.

A pre-activation reassignment edits the still-reserved candidate with a version check. An activated correction is a new durable operation: preserve the receipt and prior allocation in audit; authorize the source and target market/profile; lock all involved clients in stable ID order (including subsidiaries); preview exact source entitlement restoration and target package/price/period; reserve the same receipt against the correction; compensate the original operation's benefit; then activate the target with one surviving paid allocation and one original cash event. Keep the existing Payment as the economic event, audit any updated current client/deal linkage, cancel the old deal as a staff correction with no fraud inference, and attach a replacement deal; do not mark the cash payment provider-reversed or create another positive Payment. Preserve posting date, gross received and Finance settlement links; recalculate customer attribution/commission with explicit reversing/replacement entries so totals remain correct. Correct free-pass/rebate/notification side effects only when they can be traced to this operation; consumed or externally irreversible benefits go to a visible review step.

Before-state/after-state entitlement snapshots and an ordered per-profile effect ledger allow removing only this operation's contribution. When later legitimate purchases exist, recompute the effective profile state from surviving authorized effects and read it back; never blindly subtract N days or restore an old snapshot over newer work. Historical records without enough provenance, cross-market/currency/product-incompatible moves, bundles and consumed benefits show a review path and remain held until a safe preview exists. Same-market, same purchased package is the fast default; changed product/discount requires current authority and a refreshed preview. Proposed default for a mistaken profile correction: give the correct profile the full purchased duration from correction time under the normal stacking rule, with the service-error adjustment explicit in audit and unchanged cash. A rep can choose an authorized shorter/remaining-period option in preview. This generosity is a proposed policy recorded for Ian's review, not a claim that funds were received again.

Use states previewed → reserved → source_correcting → target_applying → verified, with per-step attempts and uncertain/needs_review on ambiguity. A failed or unconfirmed source compensation must not release receipt principal or silently activate the target. Preview and execute are idempotent and repeat execution returns the same operation. Partial success shows both accounts and exact completed/pending steps. Undoing a correction creates a compensating correction through the same preview flow; there is no destructive “forget history” shortcut. Atomic exposure policy for review-after-activation

Automatic eligibility reserves exposure in the same CRM transaction as the receipt allocation/operation. For each receiving account and currency, maintain a daily bucket keyed by (account_id, currency, local_accounting_date) and one outstanding bucket. Freeze the accounting timezone for the day's policy; timezone changes take effect next accounting day. Store policy version as audit metadata, not a new bucket key that resets spending. Use locked rows/atomic conditional increments so concurrent individually eligible receipts cannot exceed the aggregate limits.

Explicit limits: maximum single receipt, daily gross automatic authorizations, outstanding sales-unreviewed principal, and outstanding phone-only funds-unverified principal. A phone-only decision must fit all applicable limits. Exact minor units only; currencies have separate budgets. Save one budget reservation keyed uniquely to the economic receipt/operation; retries, duplicated notifications and reassignment do not reserve again or replenish the daily allowance. The initial automatic attempt counts against daily gross once dispatched, even if later undone; a clearly undispatched cancelled reservation may be released transactionally. Corrections retain the original receipt's outstanding risk until resolved and do not create a second cash budget.

Uncertain/dispatched operations remain charged against all relevant outstanding limits across midnight. Sales confirmation releases only the unreviewed counter, once; it does not promote a phone SMS to provider verification. Provider/statement verification releases the phone-only unverified counter once. A reversal/dispute remains held until Finance records an evidenced resolution. Daily gross never decreases because a reviewer clicked correct. Release events carry their own unique idempotency keys; reconcile counters to the durable reservation ledger. A limit hit produces an immediate review-ready suggestion rather than an invisible failure. Authorized staff can approve a legitimate case through the normal scoped manual workflow; increasing an automatic budget requires a separately audited policy change and does not reset the consumed daily bucket.

Test two concurrent receipts each below the single cap but together above the daily/outstanding cap; midnight carry-over; duplicated review/statement callbacks; worker crash before/after reservation; changed policy/timezone; correction with the same receipt; and uncertainty held longer than a day. Inspect the operation/reservation rows and account totals on real MySQL/MariaDB.
