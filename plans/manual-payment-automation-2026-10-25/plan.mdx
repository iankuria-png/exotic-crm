## Recommendation

The intended default is **payment arrives → identify customer → identify purchased package → activate autonomously → sales review afterward**. Use the Ghana and TZ office Android phones as private recipient-event sources. Combine messages for the same receipt, resolve reviewed payer/purchase context, reuse or create the correct paid subscription and apply one verified entitlement effect. Clear cases need no rep to copy a code, choose a package or press Activate. Sales works a review-after-activation queue and prefilled exceptions, with undo/reassignment from Payments and Client Detail. Shadow capture is a validation stage before enabling this mode, not the product's final operating model.

Ian confirmed both phones are Android and physically in the office. Ghana's configured manual method identifies MTN. The supplied TZ XML now shows **Vodacom M-PESA receiving-message families with thousands of exact TZ #6 CRM reference links**. It identifies the principal observed rail; device/SIM/account ownership and current binding must still be verified at setup. Tanzania #2 has a separate configured Vodacom method. TZ #6 is the paid primary; Tanzania #2 receives free exposure for the same paid period. Keep separate profile identities and one linked entitlement bundle, with a single paid receipt on TZ.

For example, a Ghana advertiser chooses a GHS 380 product or tells a rep which product to activate. A recipient-side credit appears on the office phone. CRM checks its account, currency, principal, finality and receipt identity, then links it to the existing GHS 380 payment claim. For an eligible clear match, the system activates once and the rep sees “Auto-activated · Review needed”. If the profile was already activated when proof was uploaded, the same receipt closes verification without adding subscription days.

The long-term system is a **collection and reconciliation layer with interchangeable inputs**: official provider events first where an existing collection account supports them, Android SMS capture where needed, and statement imports for gaps. This keeps current customer payment habits usable.

### Autonomous decision policy and historical cutover

The engine evaluates four separate questions: **Is this eligible receiving evidence? Which exact profile? Which authorized purchased package? Can the benefit be applied safely once?** A clear decision needs all four. An owned current intent is the strongest path. For repeat behavior, use an explicitly reviewed payer/profile link and a valid standing renewal agreement, reinforced by recent unambiguous history; sales does not create a fresh deal for every renewal. The system creates/reuses the quote/deal snapshot under that authority. A standing agreement specifies exact profile/market, tier/product/duration, final agreed amount/currency, stacking, validity and revocation. History alone, a public advert phone or a copied receipt code is not such authority. New negotiated prices and changed packages obtain missing authorization in the exception drawer.

Freeze the decision-rule version before time-separated shadow evaluation against adjudicated intent cases, including deliberate profile switches, shared payers, different duration at the same amount and changed agreements. Report customer and package errors independently and jointly; retained CRM labels are leads for adjudication, not unquestioned truth. Never calculate automatic coverage from reference-link rate or call the exploratory parse rate production accuracy.

**All supplied XML events are historical/reconciliation-only.** Do not replay the 2024–2026 archive into an activation worker. At live enrollment, pin the server cutover instant, device/SIM/account mapping and durable inbox cursor. Process an overlap for deduplication and reconciliation, but a pre-cutover provider transaction remains historical even if its SMS arrives after cutover. Unknown provider time or cutover ambiguity holds for review. Historical receipts can propose links and reveal missing ledger data; service recovery is separately reviewed through the existing recovery workflow. Authenticated new live delivery enters account-scoped policy only after the target WP contract/all-writer gates and caps pass.

## Capture choice

**Recommend a short SMSGate trial on the existing two office Android devices, with parsing in Laravel.** It is provider-neutral and can expose incoming raw messages with SIM context. Use a local registration/direct HTTPS webhook topology on the phone if the tested release works without a permanently reachable handset server; otherwise a small private SMSGate deployment outside shared cPanel. No incoming Internet port needs to be exposed on a handset. Pin the tested APK/version/hash and document actual data paths. Stock on-device filtering, replay after reboot, SIM identity and heartbeat behavior are acceptance gates, not assumed features.

SMSGate documents signed webhooks, retries and multipart assembly. Its webhook retries are finite (default 14, roughly two days), so the app alone is not an indefinite archive: add monitored backlog recovery and statement/inbox catch-up. [SMSGate webhook contract](https://docs.sms-gate.app/features/webhooks/).

PesaCast is a credible Kenya-specific comparison: the current repository supports direct phone-to-server webhooks, a phone queue and signed deliveries; a desktop is optional. Its M-PESA-specific parsing is a narrower fit than a provider-neutral bridge for Ghana/TZ. Research pinned PesaCast HEAD `adf92771f71e1f3f8ad8ba05360611e4fae4930b`; no executable was installed or audited. [PesaCast repository](https://github.com/davidamunga/pesacast), [webhook documentation](https://github.com/davidamunga/pesacast/blob/main/docs/webhooks.md).

SMS Enabler supports a modem/computer receiving SMS and forwarding over HTTP. It is a viable office hardware alternative, but adds a modem, computer uptime and SIM placement to a pilot with two existing Android phones. Keep it as a fallback if handset reliability fails the trial. [SMS Enabler](https://smsenabler.com/).

### Receiving accounts and events

Create a collection account identity separate from a platform: country, provider, actual receiving wallet/bank account and ownership. Maintain account-to-platform bindings with effective dates and the complete historical destination snapshot. A shared account creates candidate platforms, never an automatic country-wide grant. Enrol each office SIM/subscription to its account; detect SIM replacement, slot changes and provider changes before accepting automatic attribution. Store contact numbers and subscriber identity privately, with restricted encrypted fields. The human-entered provider label is not enough to authorize an account.

Use two duplicate layers: transport `(device_id, external_event_id)` for phone retries; canonical provider receipt `(country, provider, provider_account_scope, provider_transaction_id_norm)` for SMS/API/statement convergence. Country/currency namespace rules are explicit; currency mismatch on the same canonical receipt is a conflict, not a new spendable receipt. Reference aliases can map different sender/bank/wallet IDs only through observed provider/statement evidence or a finance-reviewed mapping. Missing/ambiguous IDs remain in review. Reversal/correction observations append to the same receipt and can arrive before the original credit.

A durable raw-event insert and processing outbox must commit before HTTP acknowledgement. A duplicated ID with changed content is quarantined and logged as a conflict. Parser success is independent of ingest success. Unknown events can be acknowledged as durably quarantined so retries do not overwhelm the queue. Store provider time, SMS-receive time and server-ingest time separately.

Monitor capture coverage against provider statement credits, eligible auto rate against all positive scoped receipts, and review rate by reason. Measure p95 phone-receive→CRM visible <60 seconds when online and eligible approval→verified entitlement <2 minutes as **pilot targets**, separating provider SMS latency and offline backlog. Do not promise real-time activation while a provider or phone is offline.

With 1,000 genuine paid receipts/month, removing 3–5 minutes of handling would save roughly 50–83 staff-hours/month. That is a scenario, not measured Exotic savings. Phase 4 measures actual paid receipts and staff time before a rollout business case; the 5,039 native records are not established distinct paid transfers and must not be multiplied blindly.

### Additional acceptance paths required by the hybrid workflow

- Same payer number on several profiles: show named choices, no first-row auto activation. A verified alternative payer renews the correct profile without changing its advert contact. A new or corrected relationship starts as review-required.
- TZS 35,000 matches both 7-day and 8-day ProductPrice rows: prefer a valid profile-bound pending quote; otherwise show both and the last agreed package. A negotiated payment below standard price can fulfill an approved quote; an unapproved discount requests authority inline. History, agent-created catalogue rows and nearest-price scores alone never bypass current permissions/PIN rules. Test discount cap, one-off versus recurring authority, outdated quote, partial instalment and closed package.
- TZ paid activation and renewal: Tanzania receives the corresponding free interval with one cash allocation; replay does not extend either site. Existing longer subsidiary entitlement survives; delayed retry ends on the pinned paid end. Primary success plus failed subsidiary remains visible and only the dependent step retries. Tests extend `SubsidiaryTrialActivationTest`.
- Payments and Client Detail show the same receipt, candidate/discount reasons, review state and correction result. Assigned sales sees a new event without handling the phone; refreshing/reconnecting does not duplicate notifications. Keyboard navigation, focus return, mobile drawer, loading/stale/empty/error states and version-conflict recovery are checked.
- Reassign after mistaken activation: source restores only this effect, target receives the purchased package, dependent trial follows the corrected identity, received cash/revenue stays single and commission attribution is corrected through audit. Test prior entitlement, later legitimate renewal, consumed grant, two reps correcting at once, source WP timeout, target failure and repeat execute. No high-risk/fraud flag is applied for an ordinary mistake.
- Undo and then reassign from an unassigned receipt: no provider refund is implied; correction history remains. Unknown historical entitlement provenance takes the reviewed recovery path. Signed callbacks/imports/submission auto-activation cannot race a held correction. Tests extend `PaymentClientIdentityRecoveryTest`, `DealDeactivationStructuredTest` and new collection correction tests without reusing destructive deactivation behavior.

Phase 3 must establish writer coordination for **assisted** activation/correction as well as automatic mode. Existing `submit()` activation-on-proof for enrolled accounts participates in the same reservation/operation contract without silently changing the market's saved policy. If WP direct staff fallback cannot honor a durable hold, that mutation route must be explicitly excluded for pilot profiles or assisted financial writes remain disabled. A human button does not eliminate the uncertain remote-write boundary.

## Verification and release gates

**The research deliverable** is validated MDX/HTML, sanitized inventory, code shapes, source-parity evidence and an initial message atlas. It is not a running collector or a proven production parser. No app tests, provider transaction test, phone installation or settlement canary has been run during this research.

**The assisted pilot** must demonstrate the real path: an authorized genuine low-value payment reaches the correct office phone → signed ingest → immutable event → correctly parsed receipt → one customer-bound claim → authorized review → existing Payment/Deal → WordPress profile/tier/expiry readback → provider statement reconciliation. Test a proof-activated profile separately and assert unchanged expiry and one financial event. Include same value paid twice, same code claimed twice, third-party payer, no intent, wrong currency, wrong receiving SIM, shared account, bank-to-wallet ID mismatch, screenshot-only evidence, blank Reference with Transaction ID present, zero amounts, fees/balances, masked payer, sandbox/test, pending, cash-out and reversal.

**Automatic eligibility** additionally requires seven clean observation days and at least 100 actual positive receiving credits for the selected account; every discrepancy explained, zero false/duplicate grants and zero unresolved uncertain operations; parser held-out examples pass; stale delivery and recovery tested; clear provider-order or reviewed payer/purchase binding proved; cross-channel/manual/WP fallback writers coordinated or excluded; persistent WP operation replay contract proven; account cap and Finance policy saved. These are release gates, not a statistical proof of a universal error rate. An ineligible account stays assisted.

Run the meaningful existing CRM regression cases using `/usr/local/opt/php@8.2/bin/php artisan test` with scoped filters for `PaymentImportFlowTest`, `PaymentReconciliationFlowTest`, `ManualCheckoutTest`, `ManualPaymentSubmissionIncentiveTest`, `PaymentManualRejectionTest`, `ManualPaymentBundleCommitTest`, `SubscriptionProvisioningConvergenceTest` and `SandboxPaymentCompletionTest`, plus proposed new collection event/matching/concurrency/recovery/settlement tests. Use actual MySQL/MariaDB for locking/unique constraints, not only SQLite. Verify commission/grant/timeline and channel-reporting totals. Build the affected UI per package.json and run the exact Payments review journey with authorized staff; per-market authorization, keyboard focus, readable empty/error/offline states and mobile drawer behavior are acceptance criteria.

Fault injection must cover crash before/after event commit, reservation, external dispatch, WordPress mutation and CRM completion. Replay SMS/API/statement versions of the same receipt across two properties; assert one principal allocation and no extra revenue or days. Test authenticated old-SMS catch-up beyond phone retry exhaustion and reversal-before-credit. Kill switch blocks new automatic decisions while preserving incoming evidence and holds on uncertain operations.

Rollback switches account/template automation to assisted/off and pauses decision workers. Keep immutable events, allocations, operation audit and completed benefits; never erase financial rows or delete tables to roll back. Back up before additive migrations; prove restore on a nonproduction copy. Reverse erroneous benefits only through a separately reviewed compensating workflow with evidence.
