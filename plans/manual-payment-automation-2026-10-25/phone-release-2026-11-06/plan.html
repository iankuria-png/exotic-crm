Android14 compatibility: the official identifier guidance recommends Subscription ID for an installed SIM and restricts ICCID to privileged/carrier/device-owner callers. Introduce an explicit android_subscription_v1 identity mode, while preserving strict legacy ICCID mode. Its enrollment saves device-local positive subscription IDs keyed by SIM slot in collection_devices.acceptance, verified receiving-account evidence and the existing recipient/carrier fingerprints. No new table is required. It is never inferred from a missing ICCID. A Subscription ID alone cannot identify a SIM on another handset; reinstallation/factory reset/device replacement requires new enrollment/cutover.

The private bridge binding saves the exact expected Subscription ID, slot, carrier and confirmed recipient; a measured phone number, when available, must also match. Startup inventory and every signed SMS carry the actual subscription ID. CRM requires the explicit accepted mode and compares ID+slot+carrier before accepting SMS; a blank/changed ID or a different device quarantines and suspends its account. When Android lacks the number, the envelope labels the confirmed recipient as enrollment-sourced, and includes the actual ID; this must not be represented as a measured phone number. Health includes current bindings, and only the accepted matching binding permits new writes. SIM changes and format drift must also pause new writes until reviewed; a later healthy ping cannot silently clear account suspension.
