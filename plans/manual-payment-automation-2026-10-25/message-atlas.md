# Exotic payment message atlas and sample collection protocol

Research updated: 30 October 2026 (EAT). TZ now has an analyzed recipient corpus; other countries retain the evidence levels below. Status: observed formats, **not a production parser specification**. This document distinguishes observed Exotic proof uploads, provider documentation, and hypotheses awaiting recipient-phone samples. No customer names, account numbers, complete references, balances, or original screenshots are reproduced here. Original five-image evidence stays in ignored, private Ops `var/manual-collection-research-2026-10-25/`.

## What the actual Exotic evidence shows

The read-only CRM inspection found 263 historical manual proof submissions, across five platforms: Tanzania #2, Ghana #3, Nigeria #4, Ethiopia #8 and Zimbabwe #14. These are customer uploads, usually sender-side. They do not establish what arrives on a collector's SIM. One latest approved upload per platform was examined; five images cannot establish the distribution or quality of all submissions. An historical admin approval does not independently verify settlement or imply that a weak-looking image is fraudulent.

## Redacted fixtures to collect and label

The following lines are **illustrative test shapes authored for the plan**, not messages claimed to have been received by Exotic:

```text
KE old importer shape:
[CODE] Confirmed.On [D/M/YY] at [H:MM AM/PM] Ksh[PRINCIPAL] received from [PHONE] [NAME]. New Account…
GH observed sender shape:
Payment made for GHS [PRINCIPAL] to [RECIPIENT] … Reference: [OPTIONAL]. Transaction ID: [ID]. Fee charged: [FEE] …
ZW observed USSD shape:
Transfer Confirmation: USD [PRINCIPAL] sent to [RECIPIENT]. Approval Code: [CODE.WITH.DOTS]. New balance: USD [BALANCE].
TZ observed Swahili receiving shape (redacted):
[REF] imethibitishwa. Umepokea Tshs [PRINCIPAL] kutoka [CHANNEL] : [PHONE] - [NAME] tarehe [DATE] saa [TIME].
TZ observed merchant-summary shape (redacted):
[REF] confirmed. You have received a payment of Tsh[PRINCIPAL] from [ROUTING CODE] - [CHANNEL] on [DATE] at [TIME]. New M-Pesa balance…
```

Each active **country × receiving provider × account type × language/template version** needs an initial 30 genuine recipient-side credits, plus examples of reversals, debit/cash-out, failed/pending, fees-only, duplicate notifications, balance enquiries and unrelated/OTP messages. Some variants will need longer to collect: rare reversals may be supplemented by clearly labelled synthetic tests, never counted as observed evidence. Reserve at least 10 separate-day examples per template for held-out tests. Thirty examples establish a starting fixture set, not a statistical guarantee of safety.

For each sample record: opaque sample ID; platform scope; collection account alias; collector/phone owner; Android version/model; receiving SIM/subscription mapping with effective dates; displayed sender ID; exact raw text privately; SMS receive time; provider transaction time; language; direction; principal/currency; fees; reference(s); expected structured fields; provider statement row; reviewer and label. Record missing fields as missing. For cross-network transfers, obtain **both sides of the same transfer plus the recipient statement row** to prove whether references agree. A payer reference with no verified mapping is a claim, not a canonical receipt ID.

TZ historical recipient examples are now supplied; label and split them into independent train/test/adjudication cohorts. Obtain Ghana recipient examples and verify live behavior on both office Android phones during setup. Approved historical proof uploads help catalogue sender formats; they cannot substitute for this step. The separate evidence CSV lists the current configured methods and unknowns without disclosing collection numbers.

## Parser rules and review discipline

1. Persist the original eligible payment message before parsing; keep parser version, raw digest and extraction spans. Protect raw evidence with restricted access, encryption and an initial proposed 90-day retention; retain minimal accounting/allocation evidence under the finance-approved schedule. Retention is a design proposal, not a legal claim.
2. Authenticate the enrolled phone; map device/SIM and provider to an account on the server. Accept only complete incoming messages. Names, balances, marketing links and OTPs do not belong in general application logs. On-device sender/body filtering is a required capability trial; stock app support must be demonstrated, otherwise use a dedicated payment-only SIM and restricted encrypted quarantine before a custom filtered bridge.
3. Use deterministic, provider-specific parsers; separate received credit, sent payment, cash-out, reversal, pending and unknown. Require decimal-safe principal and explicit currency. Preserve raw identifiers alongside provider-specific normalized identifiers. Missing transaction ID means assisted review, not a fabricated hash called a provider receipt.
4. Unknown wording is quarantined. An AI model can suggest a new parser from redacted samples for a human to review; it must not authorize a subscription or turn a probability score into proof of funds.
5. Label observed, documentation-only, synthetic and unverified examples separately. Train/test splits must prevent the same receipt appearing on both sides. Add drift alerts for unknown rate, field changes and newly masked identities.

## Code-shape evidence and the primary/subsidiary relationship

A later bounded read examined the latest 100 positive completed/expired payment rows per selected property, including all channels and historical dates. TZ #6 had 97 ten-character alphanumeric references; Tanzania #2 had 94; Ghana had 92 digit-only references; RW #27 had 87 digit-only references; Zimbabwe commonly had dotted approval-style references. Those are **shape counts, not operator attribution or paid monthly volume**. Internal references, whole pasted text, suffixes and combined codes also occur. Senegal's sample contains `xot-…` codes, whose origin was not established. See reference-shapes.json.

Ian clarified that **TZ #6 is the paid main property and Tanzania #2 receives free same-period exposure**. This explains why subsidiary rows cannot be counted as independent paid collection workload. The future matcher must keep one paid receipt on TZ and trace the Tanzania dependent benefit to the same activation/renewal/correction operation. Similar code shapes do not prove that the two sites use the same receiving account.

The CRM also stores varying commercial deal amounts/durations and sales-created packages. Those are purchase-context signals, separate from the recipient message parser. See purchase-patterns.json and the plan's negotiated-price contract. `Payment.phone` can be customer-entered contact data; it must not be treated as a confirmed provider sender identity without provenance.
