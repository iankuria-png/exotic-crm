Implementation plan

Daily payment reconciliation and market trial approvals

Give finance and sales leaders an auditable daily review workspace, with a correction loop for sales and admin or market-permitted PIN approval before free trials activate.

Status approved for implementation and shipmentOwner Ian KuriaDate 2026-11-05Scope CRM daily review, bulk decisions, evidence and correction requests, scoped reviewer grants, market free-trial authorization and shared trial UXNon-goals payment-provider or WordPress deployment; automatic refund, payment invalidation or service deactivation from a reconciliation decision; bank accounting, automatic no-revenue eligibility or commission recalculation

The working day

Open Revenue → Reconciliations. The default is today's payments, manual collection first, with yesterday and unresolved earlier work one click away. Check the receipt and sales entry, select eligible records, and approve. Send an unclear record back to its sales owner with a precise correction request. Flags remain visible until a reviewer resolves them. Denial records the decision while the payment and customer service continue unchanged.

A separate Free trials tab handles approval before activation. Admins approve directly or review a sales request. Sales may use the existing free-trial PIN only in markets explicitly configured to allow it.

The screens below are static product wireframes with synthetic names, references, counts and amounts. They are review artifacts, not live records or working application controls. The existing shell was inspected in React source and retained desktop/mobile fixture screenshots from 30 October; no current production browser session was opened.

Decisions already confirmed

Ian confirmed on 5 November that denying reconciliation leaves the payment recorded and service active. A different reviewer is required for payments a staff member recorded or confirmed; admins may self-approve. The admin exception requires an explicit confirmation and reason, which appear in history and export. It is unavailable during impersonation. Free trials require admin authorization or the configured PIN in selected markets.

The visual thesis is a calm financial workbench: existing IBM Plex typography and teal/slate language, compact aligned amounts, restrained status color, and evidence beside the sales entry. Information order is day and market → totals → outstanding exceptions → records → evidence → decision. Drawer entry uses 160ms opacity/translation; the bulk bar uses 120ms opacity; success updates use a short status announcement without moving the active row. Reduced motion removes translation and transitions. No decorative motion or delayed controls.

Daily review contract

Scope. First release covers advertiser subscription cash records across manual and provider channels; manual is the default filter. Free trials and complimentary benefits appear in their own register, with no cash contribution. Wallet consumption, zero-value grants and paid-parent/free-subsidiary exposure never become a second receipt. Other payment purposes such as top-ups, visitor unlocks, Love, Monetize and Tours remain in their existing workspaces for this release.

Cohort. Build the new query from market authorization, business visibility, subscription-purpose rules and the existing manual-channel predicate in PaymentQueueQueryBuilder; extract reusable predicates if needed rather than duplicating its source list. Do not adopt reportableSuccessful() unchanged: it deliberately excludes manual_review, while these records must be visible here. Include positive completed/expired cash entries and explicit activated-on-proof entries that claim successful service. Failed, reversed and invalid-reference records that have a review retain read-only history and an exception marker; they cannot remain approved. Ordinary initiated/failed attempts are linked operational context, not collected cash. Unknown channel, unmatched receipt, missing amount/currency, disputed attribution and conflicting references are visible blockers, not guessed matches. A customer-uploaded proof can support approval as a sales record; receiving-account verification is shown separately and never fabricated.

Receipt identity. A plain payment is one unit; a committed manual bundle is one unit with all allocations beneath it. Use (unit_type, unit_id) as identity. Do not group unrelated payments because their typed reference text happens to match. A bundle contributes its collected amount once, with allocated and unallocated values shown separately; unfinished/compensation-failed/voided bundles are exceptions. All child markets must be authorized before revealing or deciding a shared unit. Hidden children cannot be silently dropped. A free linked benefit counts zero. Source totals, review totals and exports use this same query and grouping.

Day and money. Use an explicit reporting timezone, initially Africa/Nairobi. Compute a half-open UTC interval for the selected local day. Use completed_at, falling back to created_at with a visible Recorded-date fallback marker; receiving/provider timestamps are evidence and do not silently re-bucket the row. Do not inherit the old queue's implicit baseline cutoff. A launch date bounds the default backlog; older records are explicitly selected for historical review. Late entries reopen that day's outstanding count. Native totals are grouped by currency; never add TZS and GHS into a single scalar. Optional normalized values reuse ReportingCurrencyService and show rate freshness/partial coverage; approvals and cash amounts always use native currency.

Review state. New statuses: pending, correction_requested, resubmitted, approved, denied. A flag is independent, with an actor, reason and explicit resolution. Flagging an approved record reopens it to pending and preserves the old decision; flags on a denied record leave it denied. Requesting correction on an approved record also reopens it; denied records require explicit reopen before correction or approval. Unresolved flags block approval; clearing a flag does not approve. Request correction, flag, deny and reopen require reasons. Denial is a terminal review outcome, visible in its own bucket; authorized reviewers may reopen with a reason. Totals reconcile as approved + pending + correction_requested + resubmitted + denied; flags are a subset, not an extra receipt count. Earlier unresolved work remains visible outside the current day.

Ownership and access. Admins can review all authorized markets. Head of sales/accounting are job functions, mapped to named existing CRM staff using new review and optionally export grants per market. Sub-admins do not acquire finance approval automatically. A manage_grants action belongs only to a true admin. Sales see correction requests explicitly assigned to them within their current market access; it does not expose colleagues' full queues. Identify recorder/confirmer from reliable staff audit events, confirmed_by and bundle created_by; Payment.user_id is not proof of a staff recorder. Deal.assigned_to is a routing suggestion, not proof of who recorded a payment. Unknown provenance blocks non-admin approval; admin override requires a reason. Revoked/deactivated staff and unassigned requests route to an admin reassignment queue. Grant, market, role and ownership checks apply to detail, proof, history, export, preview and commit, and are rechecked after the preview.

Decisions and concurrency. Visible-page multi-select only; up to 100 units. Preview returns eligible/blocked rows, totals per currency, source/review versions and a short-lived actor-bound token. The reviewer explicitly confirms the eligible set. Commit locks sorted unit IDs, checks current authorization, source fingerprints and versions, then atomically writes all selected decisions and durable events. Any conflict rolls back the batch with 409 and row-specific reasons; never report a partial success as complete. The idempotency key is unique per actor/operation and is bound to the exact action payload; same-key retries return the prior result, changed payload is rejected. Authorization is checked before returning an idempotent result. Selection stays until confirmed success; changing day, market, filters or page clears it.

Audit and stale approval. Hash material source values: money, currency, reference, beneficiary, deal/package/duration, bundle membership, classification, financial status and evidence revisions. Never hash unrelated mutable UI timestamps. Approval is valid only for that snapshot. Recompute on reads and writes so callbacks/imports bypassing model events are covered: changes surface effective Pending / Changed after review, with the previous approval visible in history. Explicit processing of the next action records the invalidation event; GETs stay read-only. Reversed/test/voided sources retain history and an ineligible badge outside ordinary totals. All domain events persist atomically; if AuditService::record() returns null, fail the mutation rather than claiming success. Dedicated review events survive ordinary audit-log pruning.

The sales correction loop

A reviewer chooses reason, required fields, message, assignee and due time. The assigned sales person sees My corrections with the original entry, evidence, request and timeline. They add a response, corrected-reference annotation or supporting proof, then resubmit. The reviewer sees the before/after and decides again. The same interface works for a bundle without creating extra cash.

Correction submissions are immutable proposals. V1 supports supplemental proof, notes and reference annotations in the review record, clearly labeled alongside the original payment. It never silently edits pinned purchases or provider references. A wrong amount, currency, beneficiary, package or duplicate cash record must be corrected through an authorized existing billing/recovery workflow; the review stays blocked until the corrected canonical record and evidence are verified. If no safe correction workflow exists, the UI says Needs financial correction and assigns it to an admin. Reconciliation approval cannot hide a financial discrepancy. Sales can explain or attach proof but cannot approve their own resubmission.

Requests and badges are durable in-app work records with staff-scoped query caches; no email, WhatsApp or SMS dependency. AgentTodo is personal and has no structured payment-request contract, and NotificationService is SMS-focused; neither is treated as an existing assignment inbox. New request records supply the inbox and overdue count. Any future outbound notifications require separate configuration.

Existing proof endpoints retain their present authorization. An accounting reviewer without legacy Payments access obtains a new review-scoped proof route that rechecks every unit market. New uploads use private storage, MIME/size validation, randomized file names and authorized streaming; no permanent public URLs or inline execution of HTML/SVG. Default proof masking and reveal controls follow actual access. Export includes original and annotated references distinctly, decision/reason/reviewer and timestamp; omit secrets, raw provider payloads and proof URLs, and neutralize spreadsheet formula prefixes.

Free trials: approval before activation

Add a Free-trial approval section to each market. Reuse the existing trial enablement/product/duration configuration; approval policy is an additional guard, never permission to turn a currently disabled market on. The authorization policy is separate from each caller's current eligibility checks. Proposed default is Admin approval only. The optional Admin or configured PIN mode is explicitly selected by an admin for markets where PIN approval is appropriate, including markets being developed before revenue. Store an explanation and review date. Revenue is supporting context, not an automatic toggle: the request does not define a revenue window or an automatic cutover threshold.

Reuse the existing hashed global free-trial PIN in WalletSettingsService; policy decides which markets may accept it. Do not copy its hash into responses, create a second competing secret or infer that entering the PIN identifies an admin. History says Authorized by PIN · Sales A or Approved by Admin A truthfully. The existing admin-only global PIN rotation stays in place. Add a non-secret pin_generation counter in the same PIN configuration, atomically incremented on rotation. PIN authorizations capture it and recheck before dispatch: rotation invalidates unused PIN grants, preserves admin grants and never redispatches an already-sent operation. Existing storage lacking a generation initializes at zero; compare-and-set updates prevent lost rotations. Protect the shared settings row through a NEW locked fresh-read mutation helper used by all five WalletSettingsService writers: saveSystemConfig, updateOperatorPin, updateFreeTrialPin, updateDiscountPin and updateDiscountConfig. Lock the unique IntegrationSetting row for wallet_system_config, re-read its authoritative current value, apply only the caller-owned allowed fields and persist within one transaction. Initialize an absent row under its unique key before locking, handling insert races. Unrelated saves preserve the current trial PIN hash and generation, even when their form payload was loaded before rotation; ordinary settings payloads cannot submit secret hashes or generation. PIN hash and generation update together. Carry the new generation through defaultSystemConfig, resolveSystemConfig, mergeSystemConfig, systemConfigForStorage and storage normalization without letting a projection or stale snapshot overwrite it; test both legacy and billing live-read modes. PIN verification and generation capture use the same locked authoritative snapshot as request authorization. Dispatch claim checks that snapshot again under the same locking discipline; a rotation after the operation is claimed follows the already-dispatched recovery rule. This makes rotation and authorization linearizable without storing any clear PIN.

PIN input is masked, numeric and paste/autofill friendly; verify server-side, clear on exit, rate-limit to five attempts per 15 minutes for both actor/market and IP, and return useful 422/429 feedback without logging the PIN.

Sales submit a durable request bound to client, market, operation (activate, extend, renew, subsidiary, field_trial), product, duration and source deal version. Admins may approve/decline with a reason; admin direct actions create the same authorization record. PIN authorization creates the same record in a permitted market. A reconciliation reviewer grant never authorizes free trials. The trial's existing duplicate, deposit, product, duration and site-readiness guards still run. The new record is authoritative for actor/method; keep legacy free_trial_approved_by compatible rather than reinterpreting its historical free-text values.

Enforce at every entry. Cover DealController activation, extension and renewal, subsidiary intent and retry, FieldSalesController::activateTrial, SubsidiaryTrialService delayed execution and SubscriptionProvisioningService trial entry points. Include SeoBoostController::store, SeoBoostService::activateBatchItems and SeoBoostWizard.jsx: these also create zero-cash trials using the global PIN today. Search all direct writers of free-trial deals/payments and classify every caller before release. Shared provisioning must require a valid bound authorization for discretionary trials even if a controller forgot the check. Automated same-period benefits from a paid parent retain their separate purchased-benefit provenance; they cannot be used as an unauthenticated trial bypass. WordPress-native manual grants outside CRM are outside this policy and must not be presented as governed by it.

Authorization and execution are separate. Validate the request/deal, current policy and PIN generation under lock, then persist admin/PIN authorization and a NEW trial_activation_operations row. Use a NEW TrialActivationService; do not route trials through PaymentFulfillmentRecoveryService, loosen PaymentRecoveryEvidencePolicy cash acceptance, or alter paid recovery finalization. Those paths intentionally reject noncash or finalize paid deals.

For this CRM-only first release, choose a single-dispatch adapter over the existing relative WpSyncService::activateClient / extendClient primitives. It is not remotely idempotent. Persist the command, beneficiary/market, source snapshot and operation identity before dispatch. Under the client lifecycle lease, atomically claim prepared → dispatching and commit the claim, then issue exactly one outbound mutation with no surrounding SQL transaction. Disable implicit transport/job retries for this mutation. A timeout, ambiguous HTTP result, process crash after claim, lease expiry or lost response puts the operation in uncertain; another worker must never resend it. Only a proven pre-dispatch refusal can return to prepared. A successful response is persisted before local finalization and checked against a fresh authorized profile read; preserve the site's reported expiry and trial provenance. A NEW local-only trial finalizer records the already-observed effect without another WordPress mutation. If local bookkeeping fails, replay only that finalizer. Admin recovery re-reads the original operation and profile: finalize only when recorded response/identity and effect prove the result unambiguously; otherwise remain held for investigation. A current expiry alone cannot prove which operation ran.

All discretionary callers hand off to this service after committing their request records; remove the enclosing network transaction only for these trial branches. SubscriptionProvisioningService rejects a discretionary trial without the validated bound authorization/operation context and cannot fall through to its old unguarded relative call. Paid paths retain current behavior. This design does not require a new exact-operation WordPress capability or any WordPress deployment. Automatic resend/recovery using subscriptionOperation / applySubscriptionOperation is deferred until exact-site capabilities and all writers are proven. Admin approval alone is not evidence of activation. Show Awaiting approval → Approved / Activating → Active confirmed, with Failed and Checking activation states. A denied request does not activate anything. Pending requests can be canceled by their requester. Edits to client/product/days require a fresh approval. Unused grants expire after 24 hours and are invalidated by a policy change; at execution, recheck current policy, actor status, market access and request binding. An operation already dispatched is reconciled before any replacement is considered.

SEO Boost batches. These are discretionary trials, with no policy exemption. Save a frozen batch request (market, exact selected clients/items, product and duration) before approval. Admin-only mode gives sales a Request approval path; PIN mode accepts the configured PIN for that exact batch and current generation. Admin approval/PIN must precede zero-value payment/deal creation or any item activation. A grant binds the frozen selected set; changing membership or terms requires fresh approval. Each item gets a unique durable operation under the batch request; partial completion preserves per-item success/uncertainty. Retry rechecks policy and entitlement state and never resends an uncertain or succeeded item. Existing SEO Boost batch/item progress remains visible; add Awaiting approval instead of labeling it failed. No admin self-approval restriction is applied to free-trial requests; the maker/checker exception belongs to payment reviews.

Primary and subsidiary markets. An elective subsidiary trial needs authorization for its target market. The paid parent can complete while the optional trial is Awaiting approval; present separate progress and retry states. Approval for one market cannot grant a different market or a longer duration. For an existing approved subsidiary intent, retries use the original operation identity and recheck eligibility; do not create a second entitlement. The collection automation's TZ paid → Tanzania same-period purchased exposure is not reclassified as a discretionary new trial.

Migration. Introduce policy and request tables additively. Backfill all current markets with an admin-only authorization policy without changing eligibility, PIN hash, duration or product. Capture a before/after policy manifest. Existing active trials are grandfathered and remain active; no retrospective expiration, grant replay or payment creation. Existing pending discretionary intents lacking verifiable authorization are held for review when executed, not silently approved. Keep the new policy cutover server-enforced; hiding a UI field or allowing a legacy client to pass free_trial_pin must not bypass an admin-only market.

UX states and quality bar

Desktop uses the real 256px shell at implementation scale, sticky table header and stable amount columns. Wireframes compress the shell to fit the plan. Phone layouts at 360/390px switch to compact receipt rows, full-screen evidence, reachable fixed actions and a confirmation sheet; show amount, client, owner and review state before secondary metadata. Selection is explicit and does not depend on row-click gestures. Targets are at least 44px on touch.

Keyboard users can select checkboxes, open evidence and move between receipts. Drawers trap focus, Escape closes when safe, and focus returns to the opening row. Text labels accompany every status color. Announce async success/errors, retain unsaved correction drafts on recoverable errors, and warn before discarding a draft. Honor reduced motion. Minimum body/control text 14px; dense secondary metadata may be 12px with sufficient contrast. Table loading preserves column widths. On error retain filters and offer retry; a failed fetch never shows zero collection. Empty day says No records for this day; completed queue says All visible records reviewed; zero restricted scope says No assigned markets. Distinguish missing proof, unavailable private file, stale preview, denied permission, admin self-approval, PIN lockout, missing PIN configuration, policy changed, awaiting admin, SEO batch Awaiting approval and per-item progress, and activation uncertainty. Free-trial controls show allowed actions before sales enter data.

Rollout, recovery and completion evidence

This is primarily a CRM release; fleet skills supply read-only diagnosis, exact identity, pinned artifacts and independent acceptance. It does not require copying files across WordPress sites. No fleet deployment is proposed by this plan. An approval to implement locally does not itself authorize a production migration, market policy change, customer grant or push.

Release daily review first, then trial enforcement after the trial caller matrix is complete. Keep each domain behind a server-side availability flag during preparation. Before trial cutover, preview the affected markets and existing queued intents; back up original policy and PIN configuration privately; obtain the exact authorized deployment/configuration scope. Pin application source and matching local Vite assets. Ian normally pulls via cPanel; any agent-run production step must use a registered bin/mkt action with the prescribed preview/apply/readback flow. Nothing in this planning task changes the live PIN or market eligibility.

For recovery, hide/disable daily review writes while preserving its records; payment operations continue. Trial recovery must retain the new authorization guard and admin-only ability, or temporarily suspend discretionary CRM trial creation. A rollback that restores the old unguarded field flow is not acceptable. Preserve already-dispatched operation IDs and read back uncertain grants before retry. Additive tables and approval history are retained; do not run destructive down migrations against decisions. Existing active trials remain active throughout.

Completion means a reviewer can inspect a day, approve multiple eligible receipts, return one to sales, receive the correction, flag/deny with a reason, and export a complete trace; the same cash is counted once. An admin can configure a selected market's trial approval mode, review a request, and see verified activation. Sales cannot activate a trial through another CRM route without valid admin/PIN authorization. Customer service and existing payment callbacks continue normally.
