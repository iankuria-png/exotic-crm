Evidence and intended behavior

Production read-only probes on 25 November found 91 completed AI attempts, eight incomplete responses, and no queued/running KYC work. All five open retakes have the same submitted/reviewed photo IDs but different decision versions. KycSubjectService increments review_version on every decision; KycAiReviewService incorrectly treats that as proof that photos changed. Brenda's historical approval without an AI result is a truthful state.

The queue controller took 309 ms and settings 57 ms in native server probes. Browser/network cold-load latency has not been measured. The page renders the disabled-market state before settings resolve and calls the full billing-decorated platform directory just to populate market names. The new summary response returns only enabled IDs and authorized market names, avoiding the storage calculation and unrelated billing work. Queue errors remain actionable even if settings fail.

DECISION

Ian explicitly authorized diagnosis and local implementation on 25 November. This plan records that work; production delivery remains separate. AI self-confidence assesses the model's evaluation, remains separate from face-match confidence, and never changes approval thresholds or policy.
