## Design direction

The current report puts unavailable countries first, duplicates country and website rows and buries requested cities below dozens of rows. Compact filters lead into an editorial summary, followed by two parallel ranked views. Teal identifies recorded demand; amber identifies coverage gaps. Typography and proportional bars carry the hierarchy. Native expansion reveals site details without navigation. Hover/focus feedback lasts 120ms; disclosure is immediate and reduced motion disables transitions. No entrance animation delays data.

Phone layouts switch between Markets and Profile cities without scrolling through the other list. Coverage gaps includes countries with one observed website and one missing website. Editable phone inputs use at least 16px text. Invalid date intervals suppress all three report requests and retain the last market metadata for the date prompt.

## Scope and rollback

With history includes every country with any historical events, even with zero attempts in the selected period. Mixed-coverage countries retain all website disclosures. Export CSV and Copy cities include all data in the country/site/date selection, independent of local search, coverage filter or pagination; buttons say so. Demand has Today/7d/30d presets and requires both date bounds for a custom interval. A partial interval shows a prompt and suppresses report values until completed; it must never silently present today as the selected interval. The active period is visible above the report.

Retain prior task-owned source paths, manifest and compiled assets. To roll back, restore only those source/build files through the established cPanel workflow, retaining unrelated overlays. No migrations or configuration changes are required.
